FinTech Loan Credit Risk Dashboard


This multi-page Power BI report analyzes loan disbursements, repayment behavior, and credit risk across various customer segments.

Report Sections:

1. Overview: Tracks total disbursed amounts, active loans, and portfolio distribution.
2. Repayment & Delinquency: Monitors EMI collection rates and Days Past Due (DPD) risk buckets.

3. Credit Risk & Revenue: Analyzes Non-Performing Assets (NPA) and interest income by city.
